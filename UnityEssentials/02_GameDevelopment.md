Once the initial game design is selected on, we can move into the actual game development of the game. The first thing that must be developed though is the concept document, this document A Concept Document serves as the blueprint for your game idea. It organizes your vision, target audience, core gameplay, and style into a concise and structured format. This document helps clarify your game for everyone from your development team, to publishers, giving you a solid foundation for prototyping and iteration.


# 
> #### 📗Textbook Project - Concept Document
> # 📝 Initial Concept Document – Park Clean-Up
> ## Executive Summary
> ### 📄 Overview
> - **Title:** Park Clean-Up
> - **Genre / Style:** Casual Puzzle / Exploration
> - **Platform(s):** Tablet, Chromebook, PC
> - **Target Audience:** Ages 8–9 (3rd grade), motivated by creativity, achievement, and immersive exploration.
>
> ### Vision Statement
> Park Clean-Up inspires players to care for the environment while having fun exploring and restoring a virtual park. Players learn about responsibility, cause-and-effect, and the impact of small actions in a safe, playful environment.
>
> ### Razor Statement
> _Park Clean-Up is an eco-friendly game where players restore the local park by collecting trash, unlocking new sections, and bringing order to the environment. The park is in disarray… will you help save the day?_
>
> ## Target Audience
> **Audience:** Children ages 8–9 (3rd grade), playing at home or at school on tablets or laptops.
>
> **Player Type:** Curious, achievement-oriented, and environmentally conscious young players who enjoy exploration and light challenges.
>
> **Player Experience Goals (PX Goals):**
> -   **See the impact of their actions:** Players experience a sense of progress as the park transforms from cluttered to clean.
> -   **Feel responsible and motivated:** Completing tasks provides a sense of accomplishment and encourages further play.
> -   **Enjoy intuitive, accessible gameplay:** Simple controls, visual feedback, and short missions ensure that play is smooth and engaging.
>
>   ## Core Game Concept
> ### Game Objective
>  Players explore sections of a virtual park, collect trash, sort recyclables, and deposit them in bins to restore the park to a clean and vibrant state.
>
>  ### Premise
> The local park has become cluttered with litter, and it’s up to the player to help clean it up section by section. Hidden trash and harder-to-reach areas provide challenges while teaching responsibility.
>
> ### Setting / Theme
> -   **Theme:** Environmental awareness and park restoration
> -   **Style:** Bright, playful, child-friendly visuals; clear feedback for successful actions
> -   **Environment:** Multiple park areas with gates that unlock as the player completes cleaning tasks
>
> ### Art Style
> -   Low-poly, cartoon-inspired environment with simplified shapes and exaggerated forms
> -   Bright, natural, and vibrant colors that give the park a lively, welcoming feel
> -   Interactive objects are visually distinct, with visual cues that guide player attention
>
> ### Gameplay Loop (High-Level)
> 1.  **Explore** the park section
> 2.  **Collect trash** and sort recyclables
> 3.  **Deposit items** in designated bins
> 4.  **Receive feedback** (sparkles, visual progress, sound cues)
> 5.  **Unlock next section**
> 6.  **Repeat** until the entire park is cleaned
>
