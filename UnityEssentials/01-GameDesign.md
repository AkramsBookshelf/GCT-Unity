# 📜 Game Design
> By: Akram Taghavi-Burris | © 2026


The term _game design_ often brings to mind both the game idea as well as the art and visuals. This, however, is only partially true. While there have been many interpretations of _game design_, _game development_, and _game production_, sometimes even used interchangeably, for this text, we can think of these elements as follows:

-   **Game Design** – defining what the game is and how it works.
-   **Game Development** – creating the game using tools, code, art, and audio.
-   **Game Production** – managing the process to ensure the game gets built.

> [!NOTE]  
> **Everyone is a Game Designer**  
> While one person on a team may hold the title of game designer, every team member—from artist, programmer, animator, to sound designer—makes choices that shape the player’s experience, and therefore the game design.

Starting with game design, the foundation of every project, we first define what will be developed. Good design guides decisions about mechanics, visuals, and player experience—and everyone on the team contributes to it.
